Teaching philosophy

Every concept earns a lab, a diagram, or a dataset before it earns an exam.

Abstract mechanics and electromagnetism only stick when a student has touched them — through a PhET simulation, a circuit built by hand, or a graph they plotted themselves. I redesign lesson delivery around interactive digital boards and virtual labs, then use formative assessment data to catch gaps early, the same way I once built progress dashboards for a data analytics team.
